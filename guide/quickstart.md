# Quickstart

Devin Session Board is designed for engineering leads, AI coding reviewers, agencies, and delivery operations teams.

## Fast Path

1. Open the hosted product.
2. Choose a small pilot workflow and the default plan.
3. Enter a public-safe sample that matches your team workflow.
4. Export the resulting evidence or report and review it with a human owner.
